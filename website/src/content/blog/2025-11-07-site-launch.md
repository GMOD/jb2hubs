---
title: Site launch
date: '2025-11-07'
---

We launched [genomes.jbrowse.org](https://genomes.jbrowse.org) alongside the
[JBrowse v3.7.0 release](https://jbrowse.org/jb2/blog/2025/11/07/v3.7.0-release/).
The site provides ready-to-use JBrowse 2 instances for thousands of assemblies
from UCSC GenArk and NCBI, including the main UCSC browsers like hg38 and mm39,
plus a taxonomy tree for exploring genomes by evolutionary relationships.
