---
title:
  Protein structures colored by AlphaMissense, ClinVar or AlphaFold confidence
date: '2026-10-12'
description: >-
  The protein browser can paint a 3D structure by AlphaMissense pathogenicity,
  by ClinVar pathogenic variants, or by AlphaFold's own confidence.
---

The [protein browser](/protein-browser) opens a gene, its 3D structure and an
alignment of its orthologs as one linked JBrowse session. The structure can now
be colored three ways, from the Colour menu under Advanced:

- **AlphaMissense pathogenicity**, for human proteins, on an AlphaFold model or
  a PDB entry
- **ClinVar pathogenic variants**, for human proteins
- **AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT)**, on AlphaFold models

Colored by AlphaMissense, TP53's DNA-binding domain turns red and its disordered
ends stay blue. ClinVar's pathogenic variants and the AlphaMissense scores line
up beside it, on the gene's exons.
